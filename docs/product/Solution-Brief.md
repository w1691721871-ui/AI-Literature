# ResearchOS Solution Brief

## Customer context

A building-materials R&D team needs to identify credible innovation directions while working across papers, internal material, technical reviewers, and delivery deadlines. Research discovery, source checking, and report preparation are commonly disconnected and difficult to audit.

## Business problem

- Research direction discovery is slow and hard to reuse across projects.
- Source candidates can be mistaken for verified Evidence.
- Experts need a clear review point before a conclusion or delivery is released.
- Teams need a single view of the Mission, supporting Evidence, decisions, and resulting Artifact.

## AI solution

ResearchOS provides an **AI Worker** workspace. A user describes the intended outcome; the system prepares a bounded Mission, uses approved Skills, and keeps research outcomes connected to a Workspace-scoped Evidence and review process.

The solution does not claim autonomous scientific conclusions. Candidate sources remain candidates until an authorized human validates them as Evidence.

## Execution flow

1. **Set the goal** — Create a Workspace-bound Mission from a research or business objective.
2. **Prepare context** — The AI Worker receives only the authorized Workspace, Mission, Knowledge, and Memory summaries relevant to the work.
3. **Research and execute** — Research and controlled Computer work run through the unified AI Worker lifecycle.
4. **Verify Evidence** — Source candidates are separated from approved Evidence and remain reviewable.
5. **Review** — Reviewers approve, reject, or request revision for Evidence, decisions, and delivery drafts.
6. **Deliver** — A traceable Artifact remains linked to its Mission, Evidence references, and review state.

## Trust mechanisms

- Session-bound identity, Workspace isolation, and role-based permissions.
- A single user-facing execution path: Mission → AI Worker → Skill → Review → Artifact.
- Controlled Computer actions with explicit approval, verification, and bounded recovery.
- Evidence-bound research insights; no automatic promotion of a candidate source into trusted Evidence.
- User-readable audit history that excludes prompts, chain-of-thought, credentials, and raw private content.

## Business value

ResearchOS shortens the path from a customer question to a reviewable research brief without hiding the decision boundary. Teams can reuse trusted research assets, make source support visible, and retain human accountability for material decisions and delivery.

## Five-minute demonstration

Use **Solution Demo Mode** with the building-materials R&D scenario:

1. Open the Materials Innovation Research scenario.
2. Confirm the research goal in the Mission launcher.
3. Show the AI Worker plan and Candidate Evidence boundary in Mission Room.
4. Review Evidence and the resulting Research Insight.
5. Open the delivery Artifact and show its approval-aware release state.

Every visible status is derived from the active Workspace. The demonstration does not fabricate Evidence, approvals, or completed deliveries.
