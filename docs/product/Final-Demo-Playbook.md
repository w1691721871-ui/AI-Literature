# ResearchOS Final Demo Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook fixes the FDE presentation around one governed, five-minute
scenario. It explains the real ResearchOS path and does not create sample
papers, evidence, approvals, or user accounts.

## Golden Scenario: Low-Carbon Building Materials R&D Team

### Customer Background

A building-materials R&D team is assessing future low-carbon material
opportunities. Its research sources are distributed across existing authorized
material, and the team needs a repeatable path from research question to a
reviewable research deliverable.

### Research Goal

**Analyze future research opportunities in low-carbon building materials using
traceable evidence.**

### AI Employee Task

1. Understand the stated research outcome.
2. Prepare a bounded Research Plan in the authorized Workspace.
3. Collect candidate sources without treating them as trusted Evidence.
4. Present the Evidence boundary and pause for human Review.
5. Prepare a reviewable Research Brief or Delivery only through the existing
   governed workflow.

### Expected Evidence

- Authorized, indexed sources relevant to low-carbon building materials.
- Candidate Evidence shown separately from verified Evidence.
- Source quality and review status visible in the Mission Room.

If no authorized indexed source is available, the expected result is an
explicit Evidence boundary—not a research conclusion.

### Review Decision

A **Reviewer** validates candidate Evidence and decides whether the Mission
may continue to delivery preparation. A Demo Member cannot approve Evidence,
release a Delivery, or bypass the review boundary.

### Delivery Result

The expected deliverable is a traceable Research Brief or Artifact draft with
Evidence support and a visible stage:

`Draft → Review → Approved → Delivered`

Only a real approved workflow can show an approved or delivered result.

## Demo Workspace Role Guide

| Role | Use in the presentation | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Opens the customer scenario and confirms the authorized Workspace boundary. | “The Owner sets the research outcome and keeps the Workspace scope accountable.” |
| Researcher | Creates the Mission and follows the AI Employee work journal. | “The Researcher asks the AI Employee to prepare bounded research work; sources remain reviewable.” |
| Reviewer | Validates Evidence and resolves Delivery decisions. | “The Reviewer is the human control point. ResearchOS does not turn candidates into trusted conclusions automatically.” |

The Demo Workspace session is intentionally a MEMBER-style exploration path.
For an actual approval walkthrough, sign in separately with a provisioned
Reviewer or higher-role account. Do not rename or fabricate the Demo session
as a Reviewer.

## Five-Minute Presenter Checklist

### Before the meeting

- [ ] Confirm the backend is reachable and **Try Demo** opens ResearchOS Demo
  Workspace.
- [ ] Confirm the Demo banner reports indexed sources as available if live
  Evidence will be shown.
- [ ] Confirm a Reviewer session is available if approval and Delivery release
  will be demonstrated.
- [ ] Keep Solution Demo open as the narrative fallback; it is valid even when
  the live Workspace has no completed records.

### On stage

1. **Sign in** — select **Try Demo**.
2. **Open Demo Workspace** — point out the Demo banner and role boundary.
3. **Show AI Employee** — open **Solution Demo** and explain the customer
   challenge, AI solution, trust, and business value.
4. **Open Mission** — choose the Research workflow. Confirm the prefilled
   Mission instead of claiming it has already been created.
5. **Show Evidence** — open or create the Mission, then show supporting
   sources or the explicit Evidence boundary.
6. **Reviewer approval** — switch to the Reviewer session only for the real
   Evidence or Delivery approval action.
7. **Show Delivery** — open **Research Delivery Center** and explain the
   current Draft, Review, Approved, or Delivered stage.

## Recovery Script

| Situation | Say | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No indexed source | “The AI Employee is correctly pausing before it makes an unsupported conclusion.” | Use Solution Demo or load authorized indexed material. |
| Review is unavailable | “This Demo identity is a Researcher path; approval belongs to the Reviewer.” | Switch to the prepared Reviewer session. |
| No Delivery exists | “A delivery appears only after the governed path has prepared one.” | Show the Delivery Center’s Draft-to-Delivered lifecycle. |
| Backend is unavailable | “No Workspace record was changed. We can continue with the product narrative without claiming a live result.” | Use Solution Demo; restore the backend before a live workflow claim. |

## Non-Negotiable Trust Statements

- Candidate sources are not approved Evidence.
- Evidence is not a decision without human Review.
- An Artifact is not delivered without the required approval.
- Demo content is clearly marked Demo and is never represented as customer data
  or a completed research result.
