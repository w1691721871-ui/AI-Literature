# AI Risk and Capability Boundary

ResearchOS is an evidence-driven assistance platform, not an autonomous decision-maker.

- Research conclusions require retrieved Evidence; insufficient Evidence remains visible.
- Conflict detection flags potential differences for human review. It does not decide which paper is correct.
- Computer Skill is **controlled computer execution**, not an unrestricted autonomous operator.
- File changes require a reviewable diff, human approval, verification and rollback support.
- Browser or vision capabilities marked `demo_only` are not represented as real observation.
- External connectors are governed by Workspace permissions and policy.
- Artifacts are AI-assisted drafts until an authorized reviewer releases them.
- Prompts, chain-of-thought, secrets and model-internal reasoning are not stored in audit records.
