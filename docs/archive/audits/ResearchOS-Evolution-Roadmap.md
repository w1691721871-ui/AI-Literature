# ResearchOS Enterprise AI Workspace Evolution Roadmap

This roadmap is a living prioritization record. Priorities are reassessed after
every verified evolution node.

## P0 — Context Intelligence

Move from static context assembly to relevant, authorized context retrieval.

- Dynamic selection of Workspace Memory, Evidence, approved delivery summaries
  and linked Project context.
- Ranking by relevance, trust, freshness and Mission value.
- User-readable context explainability without prompts or reasoning traces.

## P1 — Memory Intelligence

Turn persisted summaries into an accountable Research Memory System.

- Importance ranking, observed use and lifecycle controls.
- Relationships among mission records, knowledge and approved decisions.
- Verification and archive policy that preserves Workspace ownership.

## P2 — AI Worker Runtime Evolution

Evolve the unified runtime into a bounded AI employee operating system.

- Dynamic Skill planning and capability matching.
- User-readable observation, evaluation, verification and bounded recovery.
- Human approval remains the gate for consequential work.

## P3 — Artifact Intelligence

Improve reviewable Research Reports, Proposals, Review Documents, Experiment
Plans and Presentations through versioning, provenance, lifecycle and
collaborative review.

## P4 — Evidence Intelligence

Ensure every material conclusion exposes its Evidence, source, confidence and
decision trace; continue strengthening the Evidence Graph without fabricating
research findings.

## P5 — Collaboration Workspace

Advance team Workspace context, ownership, task coordination, review flow and
activity awareness using real persisted records.

## P6 — Enterprise SaaS Governance

Maintain identity, organization, permissions, auditability, policy and
workspace isolation as first-class product boundaries.
